## Market hypothesis

The market is not empty. Comparison brokers, bundled service providers and AP platforms already cover parts of the need. The opportunity to validate is an end-to-end business-premises workflow: set up → source providers → organise installation → monitor bills → manage renewals → hand off clean records to accounts.

Start with small hospitality and retail operators opening a first or additional location. Test whether opening coordination and ongoing invoice control produce enough value to justify a subscription, a disclosed procurement fee, or supplier commission. Do not claim whole-market coverage, guaranteed cheapest prices, savings, verified supplier partnerships or customer traction without evidence.
